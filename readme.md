understood, and contacted online.

## How to use it

1. Record the business, service area, review date, and reviewer.
2. Confirm the public identity before assessing local-search signals.
3. Review the website on a real phone and desktop browser.
4. Record observed evidence instead of relying on impressions.
5. Separate confirmed findings, unknowns, and recommendations.
6. Recheck the highest-impact items after changes ship.

The checklist does not produce a universal score. A restaurant, contractor,
clinic, retailer, and professional practice have different customer paths. Use
the record to explain what was observed and what should be checked next.

## Local truth boundary

Serving Fort Myers does not prove that a company has a Fort Myers office.
Do not publish an address, Google Business Profile location, or proximity claim
unless the business can verify it and the listing follows the provider's rules.

This project uses service-area language for Handers Digital. It does not claim
a public Fort Myers storefront.
