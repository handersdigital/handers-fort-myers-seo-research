# Fort Myers Service-Area Website Planner

A free, browser-based utility from [Handers Digital](https://handers.digital/)
for turning services, service areas, and a primary customer action into a
first-pass website information architecture.

The planner is designed for owner-led businesses serving Fort Myers and
Southwest Florida. It uses service-area language and does not imply that
Handers Digital maintains a physical Fort Myers office.

## Why this exists

Local visibility is only useful when a website helps a visitor choose the
right service and complete a clear next step. The planner connects those
decisions before a business starts adding pages without a role.

The method was created by [Max Tymoshyn](https://handers.digital/author/max-tymoshyn),
Marketing Expert at Handers Digital, whose Handers work covers website
strategy, information architecture, web design and development decisions,
local SEO analysis, and conversion paths.

## Privacy

The utility runs entirely in the browser. It does not submit or store the
planner inputs.
