Your first-pass page plan

A home-service website focused on requesting an estimate in Fort Myers.

  1. HomepageExplain the service area, strongest customer problem, and the path to request an estimate.
  2. Services overviewHelp visitors choose the right service before opening a detail page.
  3. Primary serviceGive the service its own evidence, process, FAQs, and next action.
  4. Secondary serviceAnswer service-specific questions without duplicating the homepage.
  5. MaintenanceDescribe the recurring need, scope boundaries, and next step.
  6. Fort Myers service areaUse truthful service-area information, local customer questions, and links to the relevant services.
  7. About and contactShow who is responsible, how inquiries are handled, and what happens after submission.

The page count matters less than the handoffs between pages.

A page earns its place when it helps a real visitor choose, trust, or act. A location page should add useful local context. A service page should answer questions the homepage cannot. Every important page should lead naturally to the next customer action.

That is also how Handers approaches Fort Myers SEO: connect local visibility to website clarity and then to a dependable inquiry path.
