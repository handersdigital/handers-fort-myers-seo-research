# Source desk

These sources support local-market context, truthful business identity, and
current search implementation checks. Review publication dates and provider
guidance before using a source in a dated report.

## Fort Myers and Southwest Florida

- [U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts — Fort Myers](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/fortmyerscityflorida/BZA115222)
- [City of Fort Myers — Why Fort Myers](https://www.fortmyers.gov/1217/Why-Fort-Myers)
- [Southwest Florida Enterprise Center](https://fortmyers.gov/2344/SWFL-Enterprise-Center)
- [FGCU Regional Economic Indicators](https://www.fgcu.edu/cob/reri/research/indicators)
- [FGCU Regional Indicators Dashboard](https://www.fgcu.edu/cob/reri/dashboard/)
- [FGCU Industry Diversification Reports](https://www.fgcu.edu/cob/reri/idp/reports)
- [Lee County annual visitor reports](https://www.visitfortmyers.com/lee-vcb/education-and-resources/statistics/annual-visitor-profile-summary-reports)

Local statistics describe the market. They do not prove a search or website
conclusion without a stated method and dated observation.
