Assign ownership across the local conversion path

A service business can rank, receive calls, and still lose the opportunity between the first click and the first useful response. Treat the path as a connected system: search result, landing page, call or form, notification, qualification, scheduling, and follow-up.

For every handoff, name the owner, expected response, backup route, and evidence that the step worked. Test the path on a phone and after business hours. A form confirmation is not enough if the message reaches an inbox nobody monitors or if the customer receives no clear next step.
