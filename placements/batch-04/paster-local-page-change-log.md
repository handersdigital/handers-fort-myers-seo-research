# Keep a change log for the local pages that generate inquiries

A small website update can alter more than copy. Changing a heading may affect
search intent; replacing a form can break attribution; updating a phone number
can leave structured data or a directory profile behind.

For every revenue-critical local page, record the date, reason, owner, changed
elements, related profiles or campaigns, expected customer effect, and the
verification performed after release. Add a follow-up date for changes whose
effect cannot be observed immediately. This creates a usable history when
traffic, rankings, or lead quality move.
