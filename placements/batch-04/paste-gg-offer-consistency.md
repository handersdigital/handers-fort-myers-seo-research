# Local offers should stay consistent across the whole customer path

A promotion can be technically live and still create confusion. The ad may
name one service, the landing page another, the form may omit an important
eligibility question, and the first follow-up email may return to the generic
company message.

Before launching, write the offer in one sentence and compare it across the
search ad or listing, page heading, proof, call to action, form fields,
confirmation, and first response. Record intentional differences. If the offer
has an end date or limited service area, attach those boundaries to the same
change record.
