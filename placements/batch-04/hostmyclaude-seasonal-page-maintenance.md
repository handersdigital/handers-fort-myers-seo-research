# Seasonal local pages need a maintenance owner

Fort Myers businesses often adjust hours, availability, response expectations,
and promotions around weather, tourism, holidays, or peak demand. Publishing a
seasonal message is easy; removing or correcting it is where many websites
fail.

Every time-sensitive page element should have a source, an owner, a publication
date, and a review date. The same record should identify matching ads, profile
updates, email automations, and form confirmations. That prevents an expired
offer or outdated response promise from surviving in one channel after the
business changes another.
