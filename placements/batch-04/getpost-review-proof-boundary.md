# A review is proof only for the experience it actually describes

Local service pages become less trustworthy when testimonials are detached
from their source or stretched to support claims the customer never made. A
review about responsiveness should not silently become proof of a specific
service result, neighborhood, or technical capability.

Keep a small review register with the original platform, public URL, date,
service context, permitted excerpt, and pages where the excerpt appears. Pair
the quote with a clear source label and preserve enough context for a reader to
understand what the customer experienced. If the original disappears, the
website owner should know which claims need review.
