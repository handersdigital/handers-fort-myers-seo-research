Build a service-page evidence register

A local service page becomes credible through specific evidence, not through repeating a city name. Before writing, list the claims the page needs to support: what the company does, who performs the work, where the service is available, how the process works, and what a customer can verify.

Pair each claim with an owner and a source. Useful sources include project photos, licenses, review excerpts, service records, team knowledge, warranty language, and clearly defined operating boundaries. If evidence is missing, mark the gap instead of filling it with generic marketing copy.
