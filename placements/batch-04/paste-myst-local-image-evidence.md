# Local imagery should carry an evidence label

An image can imply a local relationship more strongly than the caption does.
A generic Florida photograph, a supplier image, or a photo from another city
should not be presented as evidence of work completed in Fort Myers.

Maintain an image record with its source, rights, subject, location when known,
date, and approved use. Write alt text for the visible content and function of
the image, not as a place to repeat a city-service phrase. When the location is
unknown or illustrative, say so in the caption or surrounding copy.
