# Test the mobile call path before adding local traffic

A mobile local-search visit can fail long before a form reports an error. The
phone number may be visible but difficult to tap, the sticky header may cover
the service proof, or the visitor may reach a confirmation screen that gives no
idea when the business will respond.

A useful check follows one task from search result to conversation. Open the
page on a narrow screen, confirm that the service and service area are clear,
tap the primary action, complete the form or call route, and inspect what the
visitor sees next. Record the owner of every handoff and the expected response
window.
