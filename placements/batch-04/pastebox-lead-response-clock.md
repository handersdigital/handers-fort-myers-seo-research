# The lead-response clock starts before the notification arrives

Many businesses measure response time from the moment someone opens an inbox.
The customer experiences a longer path: form validation, submission,
integration delivery, assignment, notification, first reply, and the next step.
Any weak handoff can add delay while the internal report still looks fast.

Test the complete path with a timestamped inquiry. Record when the visitor sees
confirmation, when the lead reaches the system of record, who owns it after
hours, what happens when an integration fails, and how duplicate contacts are
handled. The result is an operational measurement rather than a promise based
on a best-case notification.
