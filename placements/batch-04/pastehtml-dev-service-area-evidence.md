# A Fort Myers service-area page needs an evidence boundary

A local landing page should explain where a company works without implying an
office, storefront, or service footprint that does not exist. The practical
starting point is an evidence boundary: list the communities actually served,
the services available in each area, the customer action the business can
support, and the source that confirms every claim.

That record keeps the website, Google Business Profile, paid-search landing
pages, and follow-up messages aligned. It also makes maintenance easier. When a
service boundary changes, the team can identify every page and profile that
needs an update instead of leaving contradictory local claims online.
