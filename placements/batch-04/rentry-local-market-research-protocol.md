# Use a repeatable local-market research protocol

A Fort Myers marketing decision should be traceable to more than a quick search result. Start with the service the business can actually deliver, then review customer language, local competitors, map results, organic results, paid listings, related questions, and the evidence available on the current website.

Record the date, query, location setting, device assumption, observed result types, and the business decision that followed. Separate observations from interpretations. This makes the research useful when rankings, offers, seasonality, or service areas change.

The protocol should end with an action: consolidate overlapping pages, create a missing service page, improve proof, clarify a geographic boundary, repair a conversion path, or leave the site alone when the evidence does not justify a change.
