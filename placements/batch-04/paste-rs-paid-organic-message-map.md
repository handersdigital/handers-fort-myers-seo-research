# Paid and organic traffic should not meet two different businesses

Search ads and organic results often evolve under different owners. Over time,
the ad promises a narrow service and fast response while the ranking page
describes a broad company and sends visitors to a general contact form.

Build a message map for each important service: search intent, visible promise,
service-area boundary, proof, action, qualification question, confirmation,
and follow-up owner. Compare paid and organic entry points against the same
record. Differences can be intentional, but they should not be accidental.
