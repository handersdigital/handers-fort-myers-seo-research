Define the lead-intake data boundary before adding fields

A local-business form should collect enough information to route and answer an inquiry, but no more. Extra fields increase friction, create storage obligations, and spread customer information across plugins, inboxes, spreadsheets, and advertising systems.

Document which fields are required, why each one exists, where the value is sent, who can access it, how long it remains useful, and what happens when delivery fails. Keep sensitive details out of the initial form unless the service genuinely requires them. Test the full route with a real submission rather than assuming the integration works.
