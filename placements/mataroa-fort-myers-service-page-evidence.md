# Fort Myers service page evidence record

A local service page should help a customer answer five questions: who provides the service, whether the business serves their area, what the service includes, why the business is credible, and what happens after the customer gets in touch.

Handers Digital uses this evidence record when reviewing service pages for businesses serving Fort Myers and Southwest Florida. It keeps visible observations separate from assumptions and does not imply that Handers Digital has a storefront in Fort Myers.

## 1. Confirm the public identity

- Record the business name exactly as it appears on the website.
- Use a physical address only when the business has verified and published it.
- Describe a service area as a service area, not as an office.
- Check that the public phone, email, categories, and website URL do not conflict across important profiles.

## 2. Test the page on a phone

- The service and intended customer should be clear near the start of the page.
- Fort Myers or the actual service area should appear in useful context.
- The main action should remain visible and comfortable to use.
- The page should not create document-level horizontal scrolling.

## 3. Check search interpretation

- The page should return a successful response and use the intended canonical URL.
- Robots rules and the XML sitemap should agree that the page is indexable.
- The title, primary heading, internal links, and structured information should describe the real service.
- Similar city pages should contain distinct information rather than swapped place names.

## 4. Follow the contact path

- Phone, booking, and form actions should point to their intended destinations.
- Forms need usable labels, errors, consent language, and a clear confirmation state.
- Lead delivery should be tested only when the business has authorized a test submission.

## 5. Record what can be measured

Mark analytics, call tracking, CRM routing, and revenue attribution as **Unknown** when account access is unavailable. A recommendation should cite the observation that prompted it and name the evidence needed for a recheck.
