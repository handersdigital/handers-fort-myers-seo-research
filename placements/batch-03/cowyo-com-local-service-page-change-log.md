# A local service page needs a change log

Local marketing pages drift when edits are made without recording what changed
or why. A useful change log connects every material revision to evidence, an
owner, and a public check instead of treating the page as finished forever.

## Record the decision

For every change, note the affected URL, previous claim, approved replacement,
supporting source, reviewer, and date. Keep service-area language precise: a
company can serve Fort Myers without claiming an office there.

## Check the connected path

A change to an offer, service, phone number, or response promise may also affect
navigation, ads, business profiles, forms, confirmation messages, and lead
routing. Review those destinations as one customer path.

## Verify the public result

After release, load the production page, test its primary action, inspect the
mobile layout, and confirm the search title, description, canonical URL, and
structured data still describe the same service.
