# A local marketing claim needs a proof register

A Fort Myers marketing page can become inaccurate even when nobody intends to
mislead a customer. Hours change, service boundaries move, offers expire, and
the person responsible for an inquiry changes. A small proof register makes
those changes visible before they become contradictory public claims.

## Record the claim, not only the page

Give every important statement a short ID. Record the exact wording, source,
owner, approved service area, pages and profiles using it, and next review date.
This is especially useful for location, availability, experience, pricing,
response-time, and outcome claims.

## Separate evidence from confidence

A customer quote, operating record, campaign result, and team assumption are
different kinds of support. Label them. If the source proves only that a
business serves Southwest Florida, do not rewrite it as proof of a Fort Myers
office. If a campaign produced a result once, do not publish it as a universal
expectation.

## Connect corrections to distribution

When a claim changes, the owner should know where it appears: website pages,
business profiles, advertisements, directory listings, confirmation messages,
and sales material. Update the approved record first, then work through those
destinations and record completion.
