# An after-hours inquiry needs a documented route

A local business can have a working form and still leave an evening inquiry
without an owner. The after-hours path deserves its own test because staffing,
notifications, and customer expectations change when the normal queue is
closed.

## Test the actual sequence

Submit a controlled inquiry after the stated business hours. Record the page,
time, customer confirmation, internal notification, CRM or inbox destination,
assignment, escalation rule, and the first action available to the team.

The confirmation should say what happens next without promising an unsupported
response time. If the request may be urgent, provide an accurate alternative.
If automation fails, the customer should not see a false success state.

## Give exceptions a human owner

Automation can acknowledge, route, label, and remind. It should not invent an
answer or silently classify a sensitive request. Name the person or queue that
reviews delivery failures, duplicates, spam decisions, missed calls, and cases
the system cannot understand.
