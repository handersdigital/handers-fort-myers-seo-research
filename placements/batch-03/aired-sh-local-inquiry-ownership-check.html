Every inquiry step needs an owner

A local marketing campaign is not complete when a form exists. It is complete when the business can prove who receives, responds to, and repairs each inquiry path.

Entry point

Record the exact profile, ad, or search result a customer follows and the page that should answer the same question.

Customer action

Test calls and forms from a phone. Confirm that required fields, consent language, and confirmation messages are understandable.

Internal routing

Name the person or queue that receives the inquiry, the expected handoff, and the fallback when the first owner is unavailable.

Correction path

Keep a dated record of failures and fixes. A broken notification should change the process, not disappear into a chat thread.
