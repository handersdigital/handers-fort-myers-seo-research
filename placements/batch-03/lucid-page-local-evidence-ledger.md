# Keep a local SEO evidence ledger before multiplying pages

A city page can look locally relevant while containing facts nobody can
verify. An evidence ledger keeps the page tied to the business that must
maintain it.

## Record the claim and its source

For every local page, list the service, service-area boundary, phone and contact
route, hours when relevant, proof, author, publication date, and review date.
Mark whether each item came from an official business record, a public source,
an original observation, or an explicit inference.

The distinction matters. Serving Fort Myers is not the same claim as operating
a Fort Myers office. A general service description is not evidence that every
service is available in every nearby community. When a fact changes, the ledger
shows which pages and profiles need correction.

## Connect technical and human ownership

The page also needs a canonical URL, indexability decision, internal links,
structured data boundary, and a tested action. Assign the content owner and the
person responsible for the form, call, or booking path.
