# Paid search should continue the same customer question on the page

An ad can attract a relevant click and still waste it when the landing page
answers a broader or different question. Message match is the handoff between
the search promise and the page decision.

## Trace one query to one action

Record the search intent, ad group, headline, description, destination, page
heading, proof, offer boundary, and primary action. A person searching for a
specific service in Fort Myers should not have to infer whether a general page
applies to that service and location.

The page should preserve important qualifications from the ad. If availability,
pricing, timing, or eligibility changes, the campaign and page need one owner
and a shared review path. Advertising should not promise what the operating
team cannot confirm.

## Verify after the click

Test the destination on a phone, complete the call or form path, confirm source
capture when consent allows it, and verify that the inquiry reaches the right
queue. A conversion event is useful only when the underlying action actually
worked.
