# Check the business profile and website as one local record

A correct Google Business Profile can still send a customer to an outdated
page. A correct website can still conflict with a public profile. The useful
unit of review is the complete local record.

## Compare the fields that affect a decision

Check the business name, primary category, phone, website destination, hours,
service-area wording, important services, and contact expectations. Use the
approved business record as the source of truth. Do not convert a service area
into a storefront claim for stronger-looking proximity.

Next, follow the profile link from a phone. The destination should explain the
service, show relevant evidence, make the coverage boundary clear, and offer a
working action. A homepage is not automatically the best destination for every
service or campaign.

## Record discrepancies before expansion

List each mismatch, its owner, correction surface, and verification date. Fix
controlled high-impact records before submitting the same information to more
directories. Distribution multiplies both accuracy and errors.
