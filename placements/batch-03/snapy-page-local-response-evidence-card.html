Fort Myers inquiry QA

Record what happened after the click

A conversion event is only a signal. The evidence card checks whether a real inquiry moved from the page to a responsible person and received an appropriate response.

Entry
Record the search, profile, campaign, or direct page and the exact question the visitor expected the destination to answer.
Action
Capture the device, timestamp, call or form route, consent state, and customer-facing confirmation.
Handoff
Confirm the notification, record, assigned owner, fallback, and response. Mark unknown facts unknown.
Correction
Give a failed step an owner, record the repair, and repeat the entire test rather than checking only the edited component.
