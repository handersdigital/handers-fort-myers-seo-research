Does the local marketing path hold together?

Use one real service and one controlled inquiry. The goal is to test the connection between local discovery, the page, the action, and the team responsible for responding.

  1. 01

    Verify the local fact

    Confirm the service and service-area boundary from an approved record. Serving Fort Myers does not imply a physical Fort Myers office.

  2. 02

    Open the real destination

    Follow the search, profile, or campaign link on a phone. Check whether the page answers the same question and shows relevant proof.

  3. 03

    Complete the action

    Place the test call or submit the form. A visible button is not evidence that the connection, notification, and storage path works.

  4. 04

    Confirm ownership

    Verify the customer message, internal alert, assigned person or queue, fallback, and correction path. Record the result and date.
