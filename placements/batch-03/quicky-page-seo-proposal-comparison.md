# Compare Fort Myers SEO proposals by the problem they own

Two SEO proposals may list similar deliverables while solving different
problems. The useful comparison is not the number of tasks; it is the chain of
decisions and evidence behind them.

## Ask for a diagnosis boundary

Each provider should state what can be learned from public pages and what
requires account access. Public inspection can reveal crawlability, page
purpose, local claims, internal links, and many technical signals. It cannot
prove lead quality, close rate, or response performance without operating data.

Then ask which problem the proposed work owns. Is the priority inaccurate local
information, weak service pages, technical indexation, missing proof, paid- and
organic-page mismatch, or inquiry follow-up? A generic monthly package can hide
those distinctions.

## Require a verification method

For each proposed change, record the target, source of truth, accountable owner,
expected observable result, and how it will be checked. Promises of guaranteed
rankings or manufactured proximity should be treated as warning signs.
