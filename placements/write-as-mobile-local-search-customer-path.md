# The mobile customer path behind a Fort Myers search visit

Local visibility is useful only when the next few steps work. A person who finds a Fort Myers-area business on a phone still has to understand the offer, decide whether the company serves the area, trust the evidence, and complete a call, form, or booking.

Use this short review sequence on the homepage and one important service page.

## Understanding

- Can a visitor name the service after the first screen?
- Is the intended customer clear?
- Does the service-area language describe reality without implying an unverified office?
- Does navigation use the words a customer would look for?

## Trust

- Are important claims supported near the claim?
- Is the business or publisher identifiable?
- Do named authors link to a real profile?
- Are testimonials, examples, and commercial relationships sourced or disclosed where needed?

## Contact

- Is the primary action visible without hunting?
- Do phone and booking links open the intended destination?
- Can a form be completed comfortably on a phone?
- Does the confirmation state explain what happens next?

## Measurement

- Can the team distinguish a visit, an inquiry, and a qualified conversation?
- Are calls, forms, and bookings connected to a defined measurement path?
- Are unavailable account-level facts marked unknown instead of guessed?
