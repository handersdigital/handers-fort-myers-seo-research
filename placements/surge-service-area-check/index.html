Start with what the page proves today.

Unchecked items become a verification list, not an excuse to publish another thin location page.

A location page is one handoff inside a larger customer path.

The page has to connect the search intent, the offer, the evidence, and the next action. A visitor can find the right city phrase and still leave if the website cannot answer a practical question or carry the inquiry forward.

  1. Read it like a visitor

    Ignore the title tag for a minute. Can someone understand the offer, boundary, and next decision from the visible page?

  2. Test the action on a phone

    Call, submit, or book using the same device and conditions a local search visitor is likely to use.

  3. Trace what happens next

    Confirm where the inquiry lands, who owns it, and whether the original page context survives the handoff.

What this check can—and cannot—tell you.

It records six visible operating signals. It does not inspect Google Business Profile eligibility, crawl data, rankings, calls, form delivery, CRM state, or response time unless you verify those separately.
