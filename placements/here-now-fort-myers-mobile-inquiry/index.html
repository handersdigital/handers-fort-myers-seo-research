Field test · Fort Myers

Follow the inquiry on a phone.

A local-search visit is not complete when the page loads. It is complete when the visitor can act and the business can respond with the original context intact.

Run the path as a real visitor would.

  1. Start from local intent. Use a realistic Fort Myers service question and open the page from a mobile result or shared search link.
  2. Confirm the service boundary. Check that the page truthfully distinguishes a verified location from a business that serves the area.
  3. Find the decision evidence. Locate the offer, relevant proof, process, limitations, and the next action without relying on the navigation menu.
  4. Complete the action. Call, submit, or book. Record errors, lost form values, unclear consent, and any context the visitor has to repeat.
  5. Trace the response. Verify where the inquiry lands, who owns it, what information survives, and what the prospect receives next.

What this test proves

It shows whether the visible website and inquiry route form one usable mobile path.

What it does not prove

It does not establish rankings, Google Business Profile eligibility, lead quality, or a physical Fort Myers office.
